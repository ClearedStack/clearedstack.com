The short answer is that it stays on your computer. This guide explains what that means and how to check it without taking anyone’s word for it.

## This website

clearedstack.com has no accounts, cookies or analytics, and it loads nothing from other companies’ servers. The pages are plain files.

One limit applies to every website: the server that sends you a page can see the IP address the request came from. Nothing you type is part of that.

## The tools

A tool is only listed here if it runs on your machine. The calculation, the text parsing and the file reading all happen in your browser, and what you enter is not sent to a server.

## Check it yourself

You can confirm this for any tool in about a minute.

1. Open the tool.
2. Open your browser’s developer tools. Press F12 on Windows or Linux, or Command-Option-I on a Mac, then choose the **Network** tab.
3. Use the tool as you normally would. Type numbers, paste text, load a file.
4. Watch the list. A tool that works on your machine adds no new requests carrying what you entered.

A second check is simpler. Load the tool, disconnect from the network, and keep using it. If it still works, the work is happening on your computer.

## What not to enter

- **Classified information**: never. No tool listed here is approved for it.
- **CUI and proprietary data**: running in a browser does not change the rules for the computer you are sitting at. Handle controlled information only on a system your contract and your company’s policy allow.

If you are unsure whether something is safe to enter, use made-up numbers first to learn how the tool behaves.
