ClearedStack is an open-source hub for federal government contracting. It combines a catalog of 18 planned client-side tools with an extensive library of plain-language reference guides. There is no account to create, nothing to install, and nothing to pay.

Because tools are currently in the planning stage, the site functions today as a complete reference manual and demand-driven catalog. This guide introduces the site's purpose and how to get the most out of it right away.

For an in-depth walkthrough of the site structure, keyboard shortcuts, and search filters, see [How to navigate this site](/guides/navigating-this-site/).

## Who it is for

ClearedStack is written for people who handle federal contracting paperwork themselves:

- a solo developer or consultant pricing a first proposal
- a founder of a small business navigating SAM.gov and solicitations
- an engineer or compliance lead tasked with NIST SP 800-171, CMMC, or CUI marking
- a systems administrator running software inside an air-gapped network or SCIF
- anyone tired of rebuilding complex compliance and pricing spreadsheets from scratch

You do not need prior contracting experience. The [field guides](/guides/#field) explain the underlying regulatory concepts, and the [glossary](/guides/glossary/) covers common contracting vocabulary.

## What you need

A current web browser: Firefox, Chrome, Edge, or Safari. That is all.

- **No account:** There is no registration, login, or email capture.
- **No install:** The site is a lightweight, static web application. Nothing is installed on your workstation.
- **No payment:** ClearedStack accepts no revenue, sponsorships, or paid tiers.

If you work inside an isolated environment with no internet access, the entire site can be hosted locally. See [Host your own copy](/guides/#host).

## Navigating the site

The site is organized into four main sections:

1. **[Home](/)**: An executive summary formatted after federal solicitation conventions (FAR Uniform Contract Format).
2. **[Tools](/tools/)**: The catalog of 18 planned tools across five Contract Line Item Number (CLIN) categories.
3. **[Guides](/guides/)**: The reference library organized under Section L ("Instructions to offerors"), featuring curated reading tracks and search.
4. **[About](/about/)**: The project mission, client-side architectural guarantees, and legal disclaimers.

For full details on how the contract structure works and how to navigate with screen readers or keyboard shortcuts, see [How to navigate this site](/guides/navigating-this-site/).

### Understanding tool status

Every tool in the catalog displays a status badge:

- **Planned:** The tool's scope and requirements are defined, but code has not yet been written. **All 18 tools are currently Planned.**
- **In development:** The tool is actively being built in its own open-source repository.
- **Live:** The tool is operational in the browser, with direct links to the application and source repository.

### How to influence what gets built first

Because tools are published as planned line items first, user demand determines the active build order. If a specific tool would solve an immediate problem for your team:

1. Browse the [Tools](/tools/) catalog to identify the relevant CLIN.
2. Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/ClearedStack/clearedstack.com/issues) or email [contact@clearedstack.com](mailto:contact@clearedstack.com).
3. Specify your use case, required inputs, and the governing regulation.

## What every tool will have in common

When tools ship, each must satisfy five mandatory listing criteria:

1. **Free and open source:** MIT licensed, with no paid tiers, ads, or sales gates.
2. **Client-side execution:** All arithmetic, parsing, and file processing happen in your browser. No data is sent to a remote server.
3. **No tracking:** Zero cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.
4. **Public sources only:** Built exclusively from official government regulations, standards, and published guidance.
5. **Clear operational limits:** Explicit statements about what the tool does not cover, reminding users that output is not professional advice.

## What ClearedStack is not

- **Not a government agency:** ClearedStack is an independent open-source project with no affiliation with the Department of Defense or any federal agency.
- **Not a security clearance or certification:** The name refers to a technical stack for defense technology. It does not grant, verify, or imply security clearances, ATOs, or CMMC certification.
- **Not professional legal or accounting advice:** Tools and guides provide calculated drafts and educational reference. They do not replace a contracting officer, CPA, or attorney.
- **Not for classified information:** Never enter classified or secret data into any browser on any system.

See the [About](/about/#disclaimer) page for the full legal disclaimer.
