ClearedStack is an open-source, 100% deterministic federal contracting platform and reference library. It combines client-side contracting tools, plain-language regulatory guides, and a transparent 10-worker automation pipeline—all licensed under MIT with no accounts, no paywalls, and no AI at runtime.

Everything running and being developed on ClearedStack is open to the public. You do not need to create an account or sign in to read guides, run verified tools, inspect live worker health, or propose edits to the platform.

For a deeper look at keyboard shortcuts and reading tracks, see [How to navigate this site](/guides/navigating-this-site/).

## Who it is for

ClearedStack is built for people who handle federal contracting paperwork themselves and want transparent, verifiable tools:

- a solo developer or consultant pricing a first proposal
- a small-business founder navigating SAM.gov, NAICS codes, and solicitations
- an engineer or compliance lead working through NIST SP 800-171, CMMC, or CUI marking
- a systems administrator deploying self-hosted tools inside an air-gapped enclave or SCIF
- any visitor who wants to inspect how the platform works or suggest improvements without creating an account

## What you need

A current web browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, or Safari) is all you need.

- **No account:** There is no registration, login wall, or email capture.
- **No tracking:** Zero cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.
- **No cost:** ClearedStack is MIT-licensed open-source software with no paid tiers or ads.

## Snapshot Mode vs. live cluster

Depending on how you access ClearedStack, you will interact with either the **live cluster** or **Snapshot Mode**:

| Mode | How it runs | What is active | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Live cluster** | Full 17-pod Kubernetes platform (7 control-plane pods in `clearedstack` and 10 deterministic Go workers in `cs-workers`), backed by `cs-git`, `cs-registry`, `cs-runner`, `cs-store`, and Postgres. | Live worker health, real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE), queue inspection, and anonymous `✎` edit proposals. | Exploring the live public sandbox at `clearedstack.com` or running a self-hosted `k3s` cluster. |
| **Snapshot Mode** | A frozen, timestamped static bundle (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts, guides, catalog, and verified tools) with no backend database or workers. | Reading all guides, searching the catalog, and running verified client-side tools locally in browser memory. | Air-gapped networks, local folders, or single-file offline use where no cluster is needed. |

In Snapshot Mode, interactive control-plane features—such as submitting live `✎` proposals to `hub-queue-api`, watching live `cs-09-monitor` checks, or streaming real-time worker queues—fall back cleanly to static preview state. See [Closed and air-gapped networks](/guides/closed-networks/) for deployment details.

## Inspecting worker health without an account

ClearedStack separates **cluster-private internals** from **public operational transparency**. Only the Portal (`clearedstack-web`) faces the internet, and it queries read-only `pub.*` database views (`pub_ro` role).

When you open [`/guides/#command`](/guides/#command), [`/tools/#apps`](/tools/#apps), or [`/guides/#pipeline`](/guides/#pipeline) without signing in, you can inspect:

- **Worker status and versions:** Live health for all 10 deterministic workers (`cs-01-intake` through `cs-10-notify`) and control-plane services.
- **Queue depths and item trails:** Which items are queued, verified, parked, or waiting in `needs_subtool` for a new deterministic sub-tool.
- **Sanitized error codes:** Structured failure codes such as `E_TIMEOUT`, `E_PARSE`, or `E_NEEDS_SUBTOOL` so anyone can see why an item paused.

At the same time, internal cluster IPs, node names, pod hostnames, `.svc.cluster.local` DNS names, credentials, and signing keys are stripped by `cs-store` and never exposed.

## Proposing edits without an account

Anyone can suggest a fix, report a broken extractor, or propose a catalog update directly from the Portal—no GitHub account or site login required.

To keep the open sandbox safe without CAPTCHAs or logins, every `✎` submission passes through a five-layer deterministic shield:

1. **Browser Proof-of-Work:** Your browser solves a ~1-second SHA-256 puzzle on submit to stop automated spam.
2. **Honeypot field:** A hidden form field silently discards automated scraper submissions.
3. **Daily-salted IP hash rate limit:** Caps submission volume per hashed IP without ever storing raw IP addresses.
4. **Strict schema validation:** Checks the target element against `ownership_map.json`, enforces the edit class (`DATA`, `CONFIG`, or `ISSUE`) and a 4 KB size limit, and strips all HTML or scripts.
5. **Human approval gate:** Proposals sit in `pending_approval` inside [`/guides/#decide`](/guides/#decide) and never touch `cs-git` or the cluster until the owner clicks **Approve**.

Once submitted, your `✎` button turns into a live status pill so you can track your proposal from **Proposed** to **Approved**, **Building**, and **Live**.

## What ClearedStack is not

- **Not a government agency:** ClearedStack is an independent open-source project with no affiliation with the Department of Defense or any federal agency.
- **Not a security clearance or certification:** The name refers to a technical stack for defense technology. It does not grant, verify, or imply security clearances, ATOs, or CMMC certification.
- **Not professional legal or accounting advice:** Tools and guides provide deterministic drafts and educational reference. They do not replace a contracting officer, CPA, or attorney.
- **Not for classified information:** Never enter classified or secret data into any browser on any system.
