ClearedStack is a catalog of small tools for government contracting. Each tool does one job, such as building a labor rate or marking a document, and runs in your browser. There is no account to create and nothing to install.

## Find a tool

Open the [Tools](/tools/) page. The tools are grouped into five areas:

- **Pricing & cost accounting**: labor rates, indirect rates and price tables.
- **Proposal & capture**: taking a solicitation apart and checking your response against it.
- **Cybersecurity & compliance**: NIST SP 800-171, CMMC and CUI handling.
- **Market research & small business**: codes, set-asides and eligibility.
- **Tactical & DevSecOps**: utilities for networks with no internet connection.

Type in the filter box to narrow the list. It searches tool names, descriptions and areas, so “rate”, “CUI” or “proposal” all work.

## Read the status label

Every tool carries one of three labels:

- **Planned**: on the list, not started.
- **In development**: being built, not usable yet.
- **Live**: ready to use.

Right now every tool is marked Planned. The first ones are being built, and each will change to Live on the day it works.

## Open a tool

When a tool is live, its name in the catalog becomes a link. Each tool is its own small project, so the link opens the tool itself and a **Source** link beside it opens the code.

## Before you rely on it

Two short guides are worth reading before you put a number from any tool into a bid:

- [What happens to what you type](/guides/your-data/) explains where your information goes, and how to confirm that for yourself.
- [Check a tool’s answer](/guides/checking-results/) explains how to verify a result against the regulation behind it.
