## Overview & Role

The **Page Budget Planner** (`page-budget-planner`) belongs to **CLIN 0002 — Proposal & capture** in [src/data/catalog.ts](file:///home/antigravity/ClearedStack/clearedstack.com/src/data/catalog.ts). It divides a solicitation's hard page limit across proposal volumes and sections in proportion to Section M evaluation weights, after reserving fixed pages for introductions, summaries, or counted front matter.

As explained in [Answer every requirement](/guides/answer-a-solicitation/), a page limit is a strict requirement: pages beyond the limit are commonly removed unread. Setting each writer's page budget on day one prevents last-minute cutting of scored technical content.

## Governing Regulations & Sources

- **FAR Part 15 & FAR 15.204-1 (Sections L and M):** Section L establishes volume structure, page limits, and formatting constraints (paper size, margins, font size, line spacing, and whether covers, tables of contents, acronym lists, resumes, or fold-outs count toward the limit). Section M states the evaluation factors and their relative importance (numerical weights or qualitative rankings such as *"significantly more important than"*).
- **FAR 52.215-1 (Instructions to Offerors—Competitive Acquisition):** Governs compliance with proposal preparation instructions and page-limit enforcement.
- **Solicitation-specific counting rules:** Because solicitations differ on whether covers, TOCs, compliance matrices, and resumes count toward the cap, the planner models fixed reservations explicitly so the user can align inputs to Section L.

## Inputs, Processing Logic & Outputs

### Inputs

| Field | Type | Constraints & Defaults |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `totalPageLimit` | Integer >= 1 | Total allowed pages for the volume (from Section L). |
| `fixedAllocations` | Array of `{ label, pages, counted }` | Fixed pages (e.g., Introduction/Executive Summary, or counted TOC/Compliance Matrix). Items with `counted: false` are tracked separately and excluded from the `totalPageLimit` pool. |
| `sections` | Array of `{ name, weight, minPages? }` | Scored sections/factors with relative weights (percentages or points, normalized automatically if they do not sum to 100) and optional minimum floor (`>= 0`). |
| `roundingStep` | Enum (`1`, `0.5`) | Defaults to `1` (whole-page budgets) or `0.5` (half-page increments). |

### Deterministic Processing Logic

1. **Available pool calculation:**
   - `countedFixed = sum(fixed.pages where fixed.counted === true)`
   - `availablePages = totalPageLimit - countedFixed`
   - Fails closed with a clear validation error if `countedFixed > totalPageLimit` or if `sum(section.minPages) > availablePages`.
2. **Weight normalization:**
   - `totalWeight = sum(section.weight)` (must be `> 0`).
   - `share_i = section_i.weight / totalWeight`
3. **Proportional allocation & largest-remainder rounding:**
   - Computes exact target `exact_i = availablePages * share_i`, clamped to at least `minPages_i`.
   - Floors each section to `roundingStep` (`floor(exact_i / step) * step`) and distributes remaining `step` units deterministically by largest fractional remainder (tie-breaking by highest weight, then section order) so the allocated pages **sum exactly** to `availablePages` and `countedFixed + sum(allocated) === totalPageLimit`.
4. **Word-count guidance (informational):** Estimates approximate word budget (`allocatedPages * wordsPerPage`, defaulting to `350`–`450` words/page depending on user-selected density) as a writer aid.

### Outputs

- **Volume Page Budget Table:** Columns `Part / Section`, `Working Weight (%)`, `Exact Share`, `Allocated Pages`, `Counted?`, and `Estimated Words`.
- **Excluded Front/Back Matter Summary:** Lists items marked `counted: false` (e.g., covers, resumes, acronym lists if excluded by Section L) so writers still track total deliverable length.
- **Local Exports:** CSV and Markdown table exports for immediate inclusion in the proposal kickoff plan.

## Worked Example & Verification

**Synthetic Input (from [Answer every requirement](/guides/answer-a-solicitation/)):**
- `totalPageLimit`: `30`
- `fixedAllocations`: `Introduction and summary` = `2` pages (`counted: true`)
- `sections`:
  - `Factor 1, technical approach` — weight `50`
  - `Factor 2, management approach` — weight `30`
  - `Factor 3, staffing` — weight `20`
- `roundingStep`: `1`

**Step-by-step calculation:**
- `availablePages = 30 - 2 = 28` pages.
- Exact shares:
  - Factor 1: `28 × 0.50 = 14.0` → `14` pages (remainder `0.0`)
  - Factor 2: `28 × 0.30 = 8.4` → `8` pages (remainder `0.4`)
  - Factor 3: `28 × 0.20 = 5.6` → `5` pages + `1` largest remainder = `6` pages (remainder `0.6`)

**Expected Output:**

| Part | Working Weight | Pages |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Introduction and summary | — | 2 |
| Factor 1, technical approach | 50% | 14 |
| Factor 2, management approach | 30% | 8 |
| Factor 3, staffing | 20% | 6 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **30** |

**Boundary cases verified by `cs-07-verify`:**
- `countedFixed > totalPageLimit` displays an inline error and refuses to emit negative section budgets.
- `totalWeight === 0` displays an inline validation error instead of dividing by zero.
- Unnormalized weights (e.g., `5, 3, 2`) normalize identically to `50%, 30%, 20%`.
