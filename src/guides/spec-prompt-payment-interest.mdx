## Overview & Role

The **Prompt Payment Interest Tracker** (`prompt-payment-interest`, CLIN `0001`: Pricing & cost accounting) is a standalone, MIT-licensed static browser utility generated by `cs-06-build` using the deterministic `calculator` stack template. It determines the statutory payment due date for a federal invoice, counts late days, selects the applicable Treasury semi-annual interest rate from a bundled rate table, and computes simple or compounded late-payment interest.

The tool runs 100% in browser memory with zero backend services, zero runtime AI, and zero network calls after load. It also surfaces statutory deadlines for agency defect notices (7 days) and contractor written demands for unpaid interest penalties (40 days).

## Governing Regulations & Sources

| Authority | Citation | Role in Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prompt Payment Act** | 31 U.S.C. Chapter 39 (31 U.S.C. 3901 et seq.) | Establishes the statutory mandate for timely federal payment and automatic late-payment interest. |
| **OMB Prompt Payment Regulations** | 5 CFR 1315.4, 1315.9, 1315.10, 1315.11, 1315.17 | Defines proper invoice elements, 30-day payment standard, 7-day deemed acceptance and defect notice window, 360-day simple/compound interest formulas, `$1.00` minimum, 1-year accrual cap, and additional penalty (`$25`–`$5,000`). |
| **FAR Subpart 32.9 & Clause 52.232-25** | FAR 32.904, 32.907, 52.232-25 | Implements Prompt Payment rules in federal contracts, including WAWF/PIEE electronic invoicing contexts. |
| **Treasury Bureau of the Fiscal Service** | *Prompt Payment Interest Rates* | Publishes semi-annual interest rates effective January 1 and July 1 each year (e.g., `4.75%` for July 1 – December 31, 2026). |

## Inputs, Calculation Logic & Outputs

### Inputs

| Field | Type | Constraints / Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Invoice Amount (`P`)** | Currency (`$`) | `>= 0.00` | `20,000.00` |
| **Invoice Received Date (`D_inv`)** | Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Date proper invoice reached designated billing office | — |
| **Delivery Date (`D_del`)** | Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Optional; used to compute 7-day deemed acceptance if `D_acc` is blank | — |
| **Acceptance Date (`D_acc`)** | Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Actual acceptance date, or auto-deemed `D_del + 7 days` per 5 CFR 1315.4(g) | — |
| **Contract Payment Period** | Integer (`days`) | `> 0` (statutory default `30` days; shorter periods for specific commodities) | `30` |
| **Defect Notice Delay (`days`)** | Integer (`days`) | `>= 0` (if agency notified of invoice defect `> 7` days after receipt, excess days reduce the payment window) | `0` |
| **Payment Transfer Date (`D_pay`)** | Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) | Date of electronic transfer or check mailing | — |
| **Interest Rate (`R`)** | Percentage (`%`) | Auto-selected from bundled Treasury semi-annual table based on `D_due + 1 day`, with manual override | `4.75` |
| **Dispute / Financing Flag** | Boolean | Excludes interest if amount is under bona fide dispute or is a financing/progress payment | `false` |

### Calculation Logic

1. **Effective Acceptance Date (`D_acc_eff`):**
   - Use actual `D_acc` if provided; otherwise `D_del + 7 days` (deemed acceptance).
2. **Adjusted Payment Window (`W_pay`):**
   - `penalty_days = max(0, defect_notice_delay - 7)`
   - `W_pay = max(0, contract_payment_period - penalty_days)`
3. **Payment Due Date (`D_due`):**
   - `D_due = max(D_inv, D_acc_eff) + W_pay days`
4. **Applicable Treasury Rate (`R`):**
   - Look up the semi-annual rate in effect on `D_due + 1 day` (rate locks for the entire late period per 5 CFR 1315.10(a)(2)).
5. **Days Late (`N`):**
   - If `D_pay <= D_due` or `dispute_or_financing == true`: `N = 0`, `interest = $0.00`.
   - Otherwise: `N = min(days_between(D_due, D_pay), 365)` (accrual stops at 1 year per 5 CFR 1315.10(b)(1)).
6. **Interest Math (360-day year per 5 CFR 1315.17):**
   - **If `N <= 30` (simple interest):**
     - `interest = round_to_cent(P * (R / 100) * (N / 360))`
   - **If `N > 30` (compounded every 30-day period):**
     - Initialize `principal = P`, `total_interest = 0`, `remaining = N`.
     - While `remaining > 0`:
       - `chunk = min(30, remaining)`
       - `period_interest = round_to_cent(principal * (R / 100) * (chunk / 360))`
       - `total_interest += period_interest`
       - `principal += period_interest`
       - `remaining -= chunk`
7. **De Minimis & Additional Penalty Rules:**
   - If `total_interest < 1.00`: `payable_interest = $0.00` (5 CFR 1315.10(c)).
   - Calculate **40-day demand deadline** (`D_pay + 40 days`) and **additional penalty** (`clamp(payable_interest, 25.00, 5000.00)` if unpaid 10 days after `D_pay`).

### Outputs

- **Computed Dates:** Effective Acceptance Date, Due Date, Days Late, and 40-Day Demand Deadline.
- **Period-by-Period Interest Table:** Shows starting principal, days in period, and accrued interest for each 30-day (or partial) block.
- **Total Interest Owed** and **Additional Penalty Eligibility** (with `$1.00` de minimis and `$25`–`$5,000` penalty bounds).
- **Rate Verification Notice:** Displays the effective date range of the bundled Treasury rate table and reminds users to verify against `fiscal.treasury.gov/prompt-payment/`.

## Clean-Room & 5-Condition QA Requirements

1. **Offline Execution:** Ships the historical and current Treasury semi-annual rate table as a bundled JSON asset inside the repository; operates completely offline.
2. **Strict Content Security Policy:** Enforces `default-src 'self'; connect-src 'self'; form-action 'none'` with zero remote API calls.
3. **Zero Tracking or Accounts:** Zero sign-in walls, analytics, cookies, or beacons. Any optional `localStorage` invoice log must be disclosed with a **Clear Data** button.
4. **Worked-Example Assertions & Synthetic Fixtures:** Automated tests assert both simple (`<= 30` days) and compounded (`> 30` days) worked examples, `< $1.00` de minimis suppression, 1-year (`365`-day) accrual cap, 7-day deemed acceptance, and late defect-notice window reduction. Real contractor invoices and CUI are strictly forbidden.
5. **Citations, Scope Limits & MIT License:** Cites 31 U.S.C. Chapter 39, 5 CFR Part 1315, and FAR 52.232-25; explicitly states exclusions (prime-to-subcontractor payments, OTAs, grants, Contract Disputes Act claims, and disputed/financing payments); includes a non-advice disclaimer and an `MIT` `LICENSE` file.

## Worked Example & Verification

### Fixture 1: Simple Interest (`<= 30` Days Late, from *Invoices and late payment interest*)

- **Inputs:** Invoice `$20,000.00`, Due Date `2026-09-01`, Payment Date `2026-09-21` (`20` days late), Treasury Rate `4.75%`.
- **Expected Output:**
  - `20,000.00 × 0.0475 × (20 / 360)` = **`$52.78`**

### Fixture 2: Compounded Interest (`> 30` Days Late, from *Invoices and late payment interest*)

- **Inputs:** Invoice `$20,000.00`, `45` days late, Treasury Rate `4.75%`.
- **Expected Output:**
  - Period 1 (`30` days on `$20,000.00`): `20,000.00 × 0.0475 × (30 / 360)` = **`$79.17`**
  - Period 2 (`15` days on `$20,079.17`): `20,079.17 × 0.0475 × (15 / 360)` = **`$39.74`**
  - Total Interest Owed: `$79.17 + $39.74` = **`$118.91`**
  - Additional Penalty Range (if unpaid after 10 days & demanded within 40 days): **`$118.91`** (within `$25.00`–`$5,000.00`).

### Boundary Fixtures

- **De Minimis (`< $1.00`):** Invoice `$500.00`, `10` days late at `4.75%` → raw interest `$0.66` → Payable interest **`$0.00`** (under `$1.00` statutory minimum).
- **On-Time Payment (`0` days late):** `D_pay <= D_due` → `0` days late, **`$0.00`** interest.
