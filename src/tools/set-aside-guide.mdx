## Overview & Role

`set-aside-guide` (`CLIN 0004`, `category: "market"`, slug: `set-aside-guide`) is a static, browser-only eligibility screener and reference guide in the ClearedStack catalog. It helps businesses determine which federal small business set-aside programs they may qualify for, whether self-representation or formal Small Business Administration (SBA) certification is required, how solicitation dollar thresholds route competitions, and whether a proposed teaming split complies with limitations on subcontracting.

As noted in [Codes, registration and small business status](/guides/codes-and-small-business/), claiming an unconfirmed socioeconomic status in SAM.gov or on an offer is a false statement to the government. `set-aside-guide` surfaces the exact regulatory prerequisites before a user relies on any program.

## Governing Regulations & Sources

| Citation | Scope | Rule Applied in Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **13 CFR Part 121** | Size standards & affiliation | Every set-aside program requires the business (including affiliates under `13 CFR 121.103`) to be small under the solicitation's assigned NAICS code. |
| **FAR Part 19 & FAR 2.101** | Acquisition dollar thresholds & Rule of Two | As of October 2025: **Micro-purchase threshold** = `$15,000` (generally bought without competition); **Simplified acquisition threshold (SAT)** = `$350,000` (above `$15,000` up to `$350,000`, reserved for small businesses unless two capable ones cannot be expected); **Above `$350,000`** = set aside when the Rule of Two (`FAR 19.502-2(b)`) is met. |
| **FAR 19.301** | Small Business (SB) | Self-representation in SAM.gov under the relevant NAICS size standard. |
| **13 CFR Part 124 / FAR 19.8** | 8(a) Business Development | Requires **SBA certification**; small business >= 51% owned and controlled by socially and economically disadvantaged individuals (subject to 2025–2026 program revisions). |
| **13 CFR Part 126 / FAR 19.13** | HUBZone | Requires **SBA certification**; principal office in a Historically Underutilized Business Zone and required share (35%) of employees residing in a HUBZone. |
| **13 CFR Part 127 / FAR 19.15** | WOSB / EDWOSB | Requires **SBA certification**; >= 51% owned and controlled by women, for work in designated eligible NAICS industries. |
| **13 CFR Part 128 / FAR 19.14** | SDVOSB | Requires **SBA certification** (VetCert); >= 51% owned and controlled by service-disabled veterans. Self-certification is not sufficient for set-asides. |
| **13 CFR 125.6 / FAR 52.219-14** | Limitations on subcontracting | Caps the share of government-paid contract value a small prime may pay to **non-similarly situated entities (non-SSEs)**: `<= 50%` for Services and Supplies (non-materials), `<= 85%` for General Construction, `<= 75%` for Special Trade Construction. |

## Inputs, Processing Logic & Outputs

### Inputs

| Field | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `is_small_under_naics` | Boolean | Whether the firm (including affiliates) is under the solicitation's NAICS size standard. |
| `program_flags` | Object of booleans | Underlying eligibility flags (51% ownership/control, HUBZone office/residency, designated industry) and active SBA certification flags (`8(a)`, `HUBZone`, `WOSB/EDWOSB`, `SDVOSB`). |
| `estimated_value_usd` | Number (optional, `>= 0`) | Anticipated contract dollar value for threshold routing. |
| `contract_type` | Enum (`services` \| `supplies` \| `general_construction` \| `special_trade_construction`) | Optional contract category for subcontracting cap check. |
| `non_sse_subcontract_pct` | Number (optional, `0`–`100`) | Optional percentage of government-paid amount planned for subcontractors that are not similarly situated entities. |

### Deterministic Processing Logic

1. **Size Gate:** If `is_small_under_naics === false`, mark all 5 set-aside programs as `Not eligible` (small status under the solicitation's NAICS is mandatory for every program).
2. **Per-Program Evaluation:** For each program (`Small Business`, `8(a)`, `HUBZone`, `WOSB/EDWOSB`, `SDVOSB`):
   - `Small Business`: `Eligible now` if `is_small_under_naics === true` (via SAM.gov self-representation).
   - Socioeconomic programs (`8(a)`, `HUBZone`, `WOSB/EDWOSB`, `SDVOSB`):
     - Criteria met + active SBA certification -> `Eligible now`
     - Criteria met + no SBA certification -> `Potentially eligible — SBA certification required before bidding set-asides`
     - Criteria not met -> `Not eligible`
3. **Dollar Threshold Routing (when `estimated_value_usd` is provided):**
   - `<= 15000` -> `Micro-purchase threshold (up to $15,000)`
   - `> 15000` and `<= 350000` -> `Simplified acquisition threshold (reserved for small businesses)`
   - `> 350000` -> `Above SAT (Rule of Two applies)`
4. **Limitations on Subcontracting Check (when `contract_type` and `non_sse_subcontract_pct` are provided):**
   - Lookup cap: `50%` (`services` / `supplies`), `85%` (`general_construction`), `75%` (`special_trade_construction`).
   - `non_sse_subcontract_pct <= cap` -> `Compliant` (`<= cap%`).
   - `non_sse_subcontract_pct > cap` -> `Exceeds statutory cap` (`> cap%`).

### Outputs

- **Program eligibility matrix:** Status, establishment method (`SAM self-representation` vs. `SBA certification`), governing CFR/FAR citation, and required next step for all 5 programs.
- **Threshold routing card:** Acquisition tier and default small-business reservation rule.
- **Subcontracting compliance result:** Maximum allowed non-SSE percentage and pass/exceed indicator.
- **Exportable summary:** Plain-text, Markdown, or JSON report for capture planning.

## Clean-Room & 5-Condition QA Requirements

To pass `cs-07-verify` and ship to `live`:

1. **Browser-only & offline execution:** Runs 100% client-side with all thresholds (`$15,000` MPT, `$350,000` SAT), CFR citations, and subcontracting caps bundled inside the repository; operates with no network connection.
2. **Strict Content Security Policy:** Enforces `default-src 'self'`, `connect-src 'self'`, and `form-action 'none'`. No ownership or teaming data leaves the browser.
3. **Zero tracking or accounts:** No login walls, cookies, analytics, or beacons. Any optional local reset/save state is disclosed with a clear-data control.
4. **Worked-example assertions:** Automated tests verify all 5 program branches, threshold boundary values (`$15,000` vs `$15,001`, `$350,000` vs `$350,001`), and subcontracting cap boundaries (`50%` vs `50.1%`).
5. **Citations, scope limits & MIT license:** Cites `13 CFR Part 121`, `13 CFR Parts 124–128`, `13 CFR 125.6`, `FAR Part 19`, and `FAR 52.219-14` with revision date (`2026-11`), displays a non-advice and SAM false-statement warning, and includes an MIT `LICENSE`. Built clean-room from public regulations without user CUI.

## Worked Example & Verification

### Worked Example

- **Scenario:** A small IT services firm (`is_small_under_naics = true`) is 51% owned and controlled by a service-disabled veteran, does not yet hold SBA VetCert certification (`sba_sdvosb_certified = false`), is targeting a `$250,000` services solicitation (`contract_type = "services"`), and plans to subcontract `45%` of the government-paid amount to a large business (`non_sse_subcontract_pct = 45`).
- **Deterministic Output:**
  - `Small Business`: **Eligible now** (self-representation in SAM.gov under `FAR 19.301`).
  - `SDVOSB`: **Potentially eligible — SBA certification required** (`13 CFR Part 128`; cannot bid SDVOSB set-asides on self-certification alone).
  - `8(a)`, `HUBZone`, `WOSB/EDWOSB`: **Not eligible** (criteria not met).
  - **Threshold Routing:** `$250,000` falls in the **Simplified acquisition threshold** (`$15,000 < $250,000 <= $350,000`) -> reserved for small businesses unless two capable ones cannot be expected.
  - **Limitations on Subcontracting:** `45% <= 50%` -> **Compliant** under `13 CFR 125.6` / `FAR 52.219-14`.

### Boundary & Verification Cases

| Test Case | Input | Expected Deterministic Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Not small under NAICS** | `is_small_under_naics = false`, `sba_sdvosb_certified = true` | All 5 programs -> `Not eligible` (size gate fails). |
| **Threshold boundary (SAT)** | `estimated_value_usd = 350000` vs `350001` | `350000` -> `Simplified acquisition threshold`; `350001` -> `Above SAT (Rule of Two applies)`. |
| **Subcontracting boundary** | `contract_type = "services"`, `non_sse_subcontract_pct = 50` vs `50.1` | `50` -> `Compliant`; `50.1` -> `Exceeds statutory cap (50%)`. |
